# Phoneme audio

Individual Sanskrit sound clips sourced from
[learnsanskrit.org](https://learnsanskrit.org/sounds/), which publishes them
under a [Creative Commons NonCommercial-ShareAlike](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/nc-sa/1.0/)
license.

Used here for personal study: tap a letter, syllable, or word in the app to
hear the corresponding phoneme(s).
